Why panel soiling matters

Dust and soil block sunlight before it ever reaches the cells underneath, quietly cutting a panel's output. Checking every panel by eye doesn't scale — SolarScan gives operators a fast, photo-based way to flag panels that actually need attention, instead of cleaning on a fixed schedule regardless of need.

The model behind this dashboard is a MobileNetV2 network, fine-tuned on labeled photos of clean and dusty panels.
